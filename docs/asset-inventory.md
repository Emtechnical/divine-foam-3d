- No separate clear image was identified for PU Foam, HR Foam, Bonded Foam, Soft Foam, Hard Foam and Memory Foam individually.

- No genuine individual brand-logo files were identified in this ZIP.

These are asset-coverage gaps, not reasons to invent product images or claims. Use a well-labelled category layout with available imagery, and leave unsupported individual product visuals out until genuine assets are supplied or separately approved.
