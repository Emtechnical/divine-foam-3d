# Divine Foam & Mattress — Homepage Design Specification

**Figma design file:** https://www.figma.com/design/HnwWY5MWdT9f1REF8Cucd4  
**Repository:** Emtechnical/divine-foam-3d  
**Status:** First editable desktop homepage concept and design foundations created. This is a visual direction / wireframe-level composition, not the finished production website.

## Design direction

A premium, tactile, material-first experience. The website should feel like a modern material brand rather than a generic furniture showroom. Use expressive material colours, restrained plum/red brand accents, warm paper surfaces, editorial typography, and purposeful motion.

### Foundations currently created in Figma

- 24 editable variables in `Divine — Brand Tokens` (single mode, compatible with the available Starter plan).
- Palette: warm paper `#F6F3EE`, ink `#17191C`, plum `#45243D`, accent red `#C92D3A`, champagne `#C9A66B`, cyan `#20C7D8`, sunshine `#F2C94C`, royal blue `#2857A4`.
- Typography styles: Sora display/heading, Inter body/interface, Noto Sans Gujarati for Gujarati content. Font availability was checked in Figma.
- Spacing and radius tokens for consistent layout.
- Foundations specimen sheet includes palette, typography examples, experience principles and a navigation specimen.

**Brand validation caveat:** this palette is a proposed web palette, not a replacement for or interpretation of the official logo. The official Divine logo must be placed from the original source asset and must remain unchanged. Recheck final colours against the real logo when its source file is available.

## Homepage concept sections

1. **Cinematic hero:** large editorial headline, primary “Explore Materials” and secondary enquiry actions, with a layered material/mattress cutaway visual.
2. **Intro:** concise explanation of the supplied material range.
3. **Catalogue:** six clear category cards:
   - Foam Materials — PU, HR, bonded, soft, hard and memory foam.
   - Mattress & Cushion — mattresses, mattress materials and cushion materials.
   - EPE & Rebonded — EPE Armour Sheet and rebonded foam.
   - Fabrics — mattress fabric and sofa fabric.
   - Recron Padding.
   - Adhesives & Hardware — sprayable/non-sprayable adhesives, FoamFix, 5in1, FastTack and stapler pins.
4. **Business pathways:** furniture shops, mattress makers, sofa manufacturers and retail customers.
5. **Brands:** Kurl-On, Prime Comfort, Jindal, Flexipol / Resiflex, Pidilite / Fevicol. Use exact official brand marks; do not redraw or invent them. Divine is an authorized dealer for Pidilite/Fevicol, not an exclusive dealer.
6. **Cumulative supply metrics:** 43,200+ sq. ft. comfort; 20+ metric tonnes foam; 600+ litres adhesive; 2,500+ metres mattress fabric; 700+ metres Recron. These figures are approved as cumulative historical totals, but their exact definitions should be clarified before final publication.
7. **Contact CTA:** WhatsApp, phone, email and location.

## Global navigation requirements

- A persistent Home control on every page. It must navigate to the actual homepage route, not just scroll to the top of the current page.
- A global English / ગુજરાતી switcher on every page.
- Switching language must update all authored interface and content: navigation, headings, product descriptions, CTAs, forms, labels, validation messages, footer and accessibility labels.
- Persist the visitor's language choice where technically feasible.
- Do not assume brand names, addresses, phone numbers or official registered product names should be translated.

## Asset and 3D production requirements

- Current layered hero is an editable geometric concept illustration only; it is not a final photorealistic 3D product render and must not be mistaken for an actual product construction specification.
- When the owner's image archive is accessible, classify each image and assign it to the correct page/category. Do not create a dumping-ground gallery.
- For photos with decorative rounded corners or explanatory captions embedded at the bottom, remove only the unwanted border/caption treatment while preserving the real product/photo. Keep originals and cleaned derivatives.
- Never distort product imagery.
- Do not alter, recolour, recreate or redesign the official Divine logo.
- Replace generic logo/wordmark placeholders with the supplied original logo asset.
- Use 3D to explain material layers, foam textures and applications. Do not imply Divine manufactures sofas, mattresses or furniture unless the owner confirms that service.

## Accuracy and conversion rules

- Only advertise the confirmed supply categories and confirmed audiences.
- Do not invent prices, specifications, dimensions, densities, warranties, certifications, delivery, installation, cutting or manufacturing services.
